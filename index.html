SESSION SUPPORT

Practical information

Keep this page open during the session. It’s your starting point whenever you need a link.

Do I need to install Python?

The supplied labs run in Google Colab in your browser. Sign in with your Google account and follow the setup cells in the notebook. You do not need to set up Python on your laptop.

How do I make my own notebook copy?

For Lab 2, open the Colab link and select File → Save a copy in Drive. Work in that copy. For the Lab 1 download, follow the upload steps in the setup guide.

What if Colab disconnects or has no GPU?

A fresh runtime loses its installed packages, variables and temporary files. Reconnect and rerun the setup cells. Lab 2 uses a T4 GPU; if one is unavailable, ask an instructor about sharing a runtime with your group.

Does “local LLM” mean it runs on my laptop?

In this Colab exercise, the open-weight model runs inside the Colab runtime on Google’s servers. It is not running on your laptop. Use the supplied synthetic notes for the exercise.

How does the group challenge work?

Experiment with one note, check the 20-note practice preview, then freeze your prompt for the 80-note challenge. Follow the notebook for submission limits and scoring. The leaderboard updates while the instructor’s controller is running.

EDUCATIONAL USE

These exercises use synthetic data for teaching. A valid JSON response or a high challenge score does not establish clinical accuracy. Check the extracted facts and their evidence against the source.
